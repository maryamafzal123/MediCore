# MediCore Hospital Handbook

## About MediCore Hospital
MediCore Hospital is a multi-specialty hospital in Lahore, Pakistan. Address: 12-B Jail Road, Gulberg, Lahore.
Reception phone: 042-111-633-267 (042-111-MEDCOR). Email: help@medicore.com.
Departments: Cardiology, Dermatology, General Medicine, Pediatrics, Orthopedics, Emergency, Laboratory, Radiology and Pharmacy.

## OPD Timings
Outpatient (OPD) consultations run Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
Each consultation slot is 30 minutes. Sunday OPD is closed; only Emergency, Pharmacy and Laboratory sample collection work on Sunday.
Public holidays: OPD is closed, Emergency stays open.

## Emergency Department
The Emergency department is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays.
No appointment is needed for emergencies. Emergency number: 042-111-633-267 extension 1.
For life-threatening situations (chest pain, difficulty breathing, severe bleeding, unconsciousness, stroke signs such as face drooping or slurred speech) call Rescue 1122 immediately or come straight to Emergency.

## Booking Appointments
Patients can book appointments through the MediCore web portal or by asking the MediCore Assistant chatbot.
Steps: create an account, complete your patient profile, choose a doctor, pick a free 30-minute slot, and confirm.
Appointments can be booked up to 30 days in advance. Please arrive 15 minutes early with your CNIC (or B-Form for children).
Walk-in patients are seen only if a slot is free.

## Cancellation and Rescheduling Policy
Patients can cancel a scheduled appointment from the portal or through the chatbot at any time before the appointment.
Please cancel at least 4 hours in advance so the slot can be given to another patient.
There is no cancellation fee. To reschedule, cancel the old appointment and book a new slot.
Three no-shows in 60 days may temporarily restrict online booking.

## Consultation Fees and Payment
Consultation fees depend on the doctor and are shown in the doctor list (in PKR).
Payment is made at the reception counter before the consultation.
Accepted methods: cash, debit/credit card, JazzCash and Easypaisa.
Follow-up visit within 7 days with the same doctor is charged at 50% of the fee.

## Insurance and Corporate Panels
MediCore accepts State Life, Jubilee, Adamjee and EFU health insurance, and several corporate panels.
Bring your insurance card and CNIC. Pre-approval is needed for admissions and procedures; ask the insurance desk on the ground floor.

## Laboratory and Tests
The laboratory collects samples 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM every day. Fasting tests (sugar, lipid profile) need 8 to 10 hours of fasting.
Most routine reports are ready within 24 hours and can be collected from the lab counter or sent by email.
Radiology (X-ray, ultrasound) works 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM; CT and MRI need prior booking at the radiology desk.

## Pharmacy
The in-house pharmacy is open 24/7. Medicines prescribed in MediCore are available with a valid prescription.
Prescriptions are valid until the date shown on them.

## Visiting Hours (Admitted Patients)
Visiting hours are 12:00 PM to 2:00 PM and 6:00 PM to 8:00 PM daily.
Only two visitors per patient at a time. Children under 12 are not allowed in wards. ICU visits are limited to one attendant, with permission from the duty nurse.

## Parking and Facilities
Free parking is available in the basement for patients. Wheelchairs are available at the main entrance.
There is a cafeteria on the ground floor (8:00 AM to 10:00 PM), a prayer area on the first floor, and free Wi-Fi (network: MediCore-Guest).

## Medical Records and Prescriptions
Patients can view their appointments and prescriptions in the MediCore portal under "My Appointments" and "Prescriptions".
For a printed copy of medical records, apply at the records office with your CNIC; copies are ready within 2 working days.

## Choosing the Right Department
Heart problems, chest discomfort, high blood pressure: Cardiology.
Skin rashes, acne, allergies, hair and nail problems: Dermatology.
Fever, flu, cough, stomach issues, general checkups, diabetes follow-up: General Medicine.
Children under 14: Pediatrics.
Bone, joint, back pain, fractures, sports injuries: Orthopedics.
If unsure, start with General Medicine; the doctor will refer you if needed.

## Using the MediCore Portal
Patients: book and cancel appointments, view prescriptions, update profile, chat with the assistant.
Doctors: view their schedule, mark appointments completed or cancelled, write prescriptions, view patient profiles.
Admins: view hospital statistics, manage users (activate or deactivate), view all appointments, doctors and patients.
Forgot password: contact reception to reset it.

## Privacy
Patient data is private. Doctors can see profiles of patients for care purposes, and patients can only see their own records.
The MediCore Assistant only accesses data the logged-in user is allowed to see.
